 		 Intel Itanium (IA-64) Support

This page is to document the planned Intel Itanium port of Windows Glass, based on Windows XP 64-Bit Edition Version 2003.

The short answer, if you're looking for one on if this will be done, is "I do not know" due to the following factors:

  • Despite Itanium Emulation now being possible, it is slow as hell in my experience, GUI Mode setup can take literal hours to complete
  • One-Core-API is most likely NOT going to get an Intel Itanium port, unless plans change to use VS2010 with it, I do not see it likely that they will add support
  • IA-64, despite me finding it cool, is also an absolute pain in the ass to work with, the hardware is sparse and expensive, with motherboards costing more than the CPUs themselves (WHICH IS INSANE) and due to it's EPIC (Explicitly Parallel Instruction Computing) nature which has never been seen since, it is even more painful to emulate
  • No Pre-Reset IA-64 Longhorn builds that use the PE are known to exist, now while there may be one around 4083 or 4084 that has this based on the AMD64 Compile of 4083 having a PE Based setup, it is yet to be documented, and more importantly for me, leaked in binary form.
  • Slipstreaming SP2 and then the final updates on top to the base ISO target would be a pain that I can't even do from an x86_64 (AMD64) system, and then nLite was never designed to support IA-64 ISOs

TL;DR: IA-64 is a pain in the ass to work with and emulate, nLite doesn't support IA-64 ISOs, One-Core has No IA-64 support, and no PE Based setup compile of pre-Reset Longhorn for IA-64 has leaked, so it is unlikely.
